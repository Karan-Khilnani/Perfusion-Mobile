# PERFUSION – PAYMENT, BILLING & FINANCE (FINAL SPEC)

## 1. Billing Model

* Pay-per-use only:

  * Lab tests
  * Teleconsultations
  * Teleradiology
* No subscriptions

---

## 2. Pricing Logic

* Provider sets base price
* Perfusion adds margin

  * Default margin auto-applied
  * Admin can edit margin
  * Admin can manually edit final price
* Final price = base price + margin
* Price locked at booking

---

## 3. Invoice & Payment

* Invoice generated for every service
* Payment options:

  * Pay Now
  * Pay Later
* Default Pay Later tenure = 1 month
* Partial payment allowed
* Full payment must be completed within 1 month billing cycle

---

## 4. Service Disruption

* If invoice not fully paid within 1 month:

  * Account automatically disabled
* Admin can:

  * Disable anytime
  * Extend due date
  * Re-enable after payment or override

---

## 5. Money Split & Settlement

* Provider receives base price
* Perfusion retains margin
* Provider payout only after customer payment

---

## 6. Billing Section (For All Roles)

Common section name: Billing

### Provider (Lab / Doctor / Radiologist)

* Invoices for delivered services
* Revenue earned (base price)
* Paid vs pending
* Date range filter
* Export PDF / Excel

### Seeker Hospital

* Invoices for services consumed
* Total cost incurred
* Paid / outstanding
* Due dates
* Pay now / pay later

### Admin (Perfusion)

* All invoices
* Payment status
* Outstanding & overdue
