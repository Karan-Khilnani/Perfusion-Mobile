Role-based differences

  • Public users can discover consultants and tests but must authenticate to book.
  • Care Seekers can create bookings, pay, view owned orders and clinical artifacts, and enter eligible video rooms.
  • Providers see operational data by provider type; consultants receive consultation workflow while labs, hospitals and teleradiology receive different dashboards and service controls.
  • Consultant and teleradiology provider layouts hide and redirect Services in the current implementation.
  • Administrators can manage platform records, catalog, approvals, billing, analytics, agreements and diagnostics; record details are generally dialogs rather than routes.
  • The video room is shared, but provider-side clinical summary/signing capabilities differ from seeker access.

Important conditional states

  • Unauthenticated protected-route access redirects to login.
  • Login can branch to email verification, pending approval, agreement, redirect target or role dashboard.
  • Provider onboarding is bypassed when a provider record already exists.
  • Agreement acceptance is forced when the backend reports a newer/current version is required.
  • Booking actions vary by service type, booking status, appointment window, room availability and post-consultation permissions.
  • Payment and booking confirmations are conditional states inside booking routes, not standalone pages.
  • Signed clinical summaries become locked; drafts remain editable.
  • Provider navigation and data vary by provider type.
  • Admin and provider mutations expose pending, success and destructive error feedback.
  • Files, reports, receipts and PDFs can open inline, fullscreen, downloaded or in a new tab depending on type and viewport.

Concise end-to-end journeys

Public and authentication: Landing or compact Home → consultant/lab discovery → Login or Registration → Role Selection/Profile Completion when required → Email Verification → Pending Approval when required → Agreement when required → role dashboard.

Care Seeker: Dashboard → consultation or lab discovery → in-page detail/patient flow → booking route with slot, clinical details and payment → confirmation state → Orders → booking detail, prescription/report/download or Video Room; Billing and Profile remain sidebar destinations.

Care Provider: Role selection/profile setup → Provider Onboarding → provider-type Dashboard → Bookings → patient/files/report or consultation summary subflows → Video Room → completion and signed documentation; Services, Billing and Profile remain sidebar destinations.

Administrator: Admin Dashboard → sidebar-selected operational list → record dialog/detail overlay → create, approve, update, download or diagnostic action → remain on list/dashboard. No parameterized admin detail routes are currently registered.

A concept requested in the brief is labeled as a dialog, sheet, embedded section or conditional state when it does not have an independent URL. Backend authorization cannot be proven by visual code inspection alone; this document records visible route gates and endpoint-driven UI behavior.

Role-based differences

  • Public users can discover consultants and tests but must authenticate to book.
  • Care Seekers can create bookings, pay, view owned orders and clinical artifacts, and enter eligible video rooms.
  • Providers see operational data by provider type; consultants receive consultation workflow while labs, hospitals and teleradiology receive different dashboards and service controls.
  • Consultant and teleradiology provider layouts hide and redirect Services in the current implementation.
  • Administrators can manage platform records, catalog, approvals, billing, analytics, agreements and diagnostics; record details are generally dialogs rather than routes.
  • The video room is shared, but provider-side clinical summary/signing capabilities differ from seeker access.

Important conditional states

  • Unauthenticated protected-route access redirects to login.
  • Login can branch to email verification, pending approval, agreement, redirect target or role dashboard.
  • Provider onboarding is bypassed when a provider record already exists.
  • Agreement acceptance is forced when the backend reports a newer/current version is required.
  • Booking actions vary by service type, booking status, appointment window, room availability and post-consultation permissions.
  • Payment and booking confirmations are conditional states inside booking routes, not standalone pages.
  • Signed clinical summaries become locked; drafts remain editable.
  • Provider navigation and data vary by provider type.
  • Admin and provider mutations expose pending, success and destructive error feedback.
  • Files, reports, receipts and PDFs can open inline, fullscreen, downloaded or in a new tab depending on type and viewport.

Concise end-to-end journeys

Public and authentication: Landing or compact Home → consultant/lab discovery → Login or Registration → Role Selection/Profile Completion when required → Email Verification → Pending Approval when required → Agreement when required → role dashboard.

Care Seeker: Dashboard → consultation or lab discovery → in-page detail/patient flow → booking route with slot, clinical details and payment → confirmation state → Orders → booking detail, prescription/report/download or Video Room; Billing and Profile remain sidebar destinations.

Care Provider: Role selection/profile setup → Provider Onboarding → provider-type Dashboard → Bookings → patient/files/report or consultation summary subflows → Video Room → completion and signed documentation; Services, Billing and Profile remain sidebar destinations.

Administrator: Admin Dashboard → sidebar-selected operational list → record dialog/detail overlay → create, approve, update, download or diagnostic action → remain on list/dashboard. No parameterized admin detail routes are currently registered.
