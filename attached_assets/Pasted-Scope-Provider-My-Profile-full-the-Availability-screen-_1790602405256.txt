B. Availability screen (reached only from that row), as in perfusion-availability-cp19.html:

Status card: Available or Paused with one Pause/Resume button. It flips instantly, with no dialog, and existing bookings are untouched.
Weekly list: seven day cards, Monday to Sunday. Each has an on/off switch and expands to its time windows. Turning a day off keeps its windows.
Windows: tap a start or end time to open a time picker in 30-minute steps. The end must be after the start, and a window can't cross midnight, so a night shift is two windows. Each window has a delete ✕ and there is an "Add time window" row.
Copy to all days: overwrites the other six days' windows with the source day's and switches them on. Days stay individually editable afterward.
Long-press a window: pauses or resumes it. It stays paused until toggled again and is not offered to seekers. This is different from deleting the window.
Saving: every change saves immediately, with no Save button. On failure the row reverts and shows an error.
Fixed values: all times are IST and slots are 30 minutes, with no duration picker.
