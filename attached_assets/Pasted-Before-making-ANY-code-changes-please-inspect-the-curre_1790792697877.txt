Problem we are trying to solve:

One Seeker Hospital has ONE hospital account, but that account may be used by multiple people across different locations/shifts — e.g. MICU, ER, Ward. A consultation booking can be created by any of them.

- ER doctor books Consultation A.
- MICU doctor books Consultation B.
- Provider later calls Consultation A.

- The same hospital account may therefore have multiple registered users/devices.
- Different bookings can have different callback users/devices.
- When a doctor leaves a shift, the callback recipient for their active bookings may need to be changed to another user/device.
