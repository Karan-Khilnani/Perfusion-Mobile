## What's actually changing, and why your reasoning is right

The spec as written has Callback Device as **one setting per account**, chosen at first login. That breaks the moment a hospital has two *simultaneous* ongoing consultations in different physical locations — ER and ICU can't both route to the same one phone. So this needs to split into two layers:

- **Device directory** (account-level, unchanged): named devices registered once — "Dr. Rohan's S24 Ultra," "ICU Nursing Station iPad" — still set up at first login or added later in Settings.
- **Device assignment** (new — per consultation): *which* directory entry handles *this specific patient's* calls, chosen at booking time, changeable afterward from that patient's own Case File.

Registration stays global. Assignment becomes per-case. That's the fix.
