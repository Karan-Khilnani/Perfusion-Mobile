==================================================
1. THE CORE CONSULTATION LIFECYCLE
==================================================

There must be ONE single consultation status lifecycle:

SCHEDULED
    ↓
ONGOING / PAUSED
    ↓
COMPLETED

These are NOT three independent buttons.

The consultation must progress based on the scheduled consultation start time and the 24-hour active window.

The lifecycle must work as follows:

A. BEFORE THE SCHEDULED TIME
Status = SCHEDULED

B. WHEN THE SCHEDULED TIME IS REACHED
Status changes from SCHEDULED to ONGOING.

At this point:
- Provider can control the consultation between ONGOING and PAUSED.
- Calling becomes available.
- Seeker can contact/call the Provider.
- Seeker can use the case-file messaging/chat service.

C. DURING THE 24-HOUR WINDOW
The consultation remains active for 24 hours from the scheduled consultation start time.

During this period:

ONGOING:
- Provider can pause the consultation.
- Calling is enabled.
- Chat/messaging is enabled for the Seeker and Provider.

PAUSED:
- Provider can resume the consultation.
- Calling is disabled for the Seeker.
- Chat/messaging remains enabled.
- The consultation itself must NOT become completed just because it is paused.

D. AFTER 24 HOURS
Status becomes COMPLETED.

At this point:
- Calling must be disabled.
- Chat/messaging service must be disabled.
- The Provider must no longer be able to toggle Ongoing/Paused.
- The Seeker must not be able to initiate calling or messaging.
- The consultation should be treated as completed/read-only according to the existing application architecture.
- Do not delete any consultation data or historical messages.

IMPORTANT:
The 24-hour period is based on the consultation's actual scheduled start date/time, NOT 24 hours from when the Provider happens to open the screen.

==================================================

ONGOING ↔ PAUSED

After 24 hours:

- Provider can no longer change the status.
