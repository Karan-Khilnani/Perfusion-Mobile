1. Logo Visibility

Increase the Perfusion logo size on the homepage to at least 2× the current size.

Increase the logo size on all inner pages (where it appears in the header) by 4–5×.

The logo is currently too small and not clearly visible; it should be prominent and readable across the application.

2. Rename and Reorganize Modules

Rename “Critical Care” to “Emergency & Critical Care” across the platform.

On the User Portal, the primary modules should be displayed at the same level and parallel to each other:

Labs

Consultation

Emergency & Critical Care

Referral

Move “My Orders” away from the main module section.

It can be placed in the top navigation bar or at the bottom of the page, wherever best fits the UI.

It should NOT appear alongside Labs, Consultation, Emergency & Critical Care, and Referral.

3. Add a New Module: Referral

Add a new module named “Referral” with an ambulance icon.

The Referral module should be built in the same structural manner as Labs, Consultation, and Emergency & Critical Care.

4. Referral Module – User Flow
Step 1: Hospital Selection

User first selects:

Hospital

Hospital location

Hospital list must support multiple filters, all selectable simultaneously:

Department (e.g., Cardiology, Neurology, ICU)

Diagnosis

Support required:

Mechanical ventilation

ECMO

CRRT

The more filters/tags applied, the more refined the hospital list becomes.

Filters must allow multiple selections, not single-choice only.

Step 2: Transport Selection

After selecting the hospital, the user selects a transport service.

Ambulance options should be shown based on the selected hospital location.

Step 3: Transport Booking Details

While booking ambulance transport, user must enter:

Patient details

Diagnosis

Patient condition:

Stable

Borderline

Critical

Type of ambulance:

BLS

ALS

Mode of transport:

Road ambulance

Air ambulance

Option to select:

Cloud physician support during transport (Yes / No)

Note:
While booking transport, the selected hospital name should be auto-filled and locked, as it was chosen in Step 1.

5. Provider Portal – Referral & Transport

Referral and transport providers should have access similar to Labs and Consultation providers.

A provider/facility can offer one, multiple, or all of the following services:

Labs

Consultation

Emergency & Critical Care

Referral

Transport

6. Summary of Intent (for Dev Clarity)

Referral is a first-class module, not a sub-feature.

Hospital selection comes before ambulance selection.

Filters are multi-select, not exclusive.

Transport booking is hospital-linked by default.

Emergency & Critical Care is a 24/7 decision-support service, not just listing.
